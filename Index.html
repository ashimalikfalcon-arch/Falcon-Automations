About Us

Precision automation, delivered with integrity

Falcon Automation & Control Pvt. Ltd. is an industrial automation and control solutions company. We help manufacturers modernise machines and production lines with reliable control systems, intelligent monitoring and expert engineering support.

From the first site survey to commissioning and after-sales service, our engineers work as an extension of your team — selecting the right hardware, writing clean and maintainable control logic, and building systems that operators trust.
